BillerQ Core Features

A guide to the primary business modules available inside BillerQ Subscriber Management.

📊

Realtime Dashboard

Displays essential metrics including the Revenue Meter (Invoice vs. Due vs. Collections), Connection counts by subscriber type (Cable TV, Broadband, IPTV), and Monthly Overview metrics.

Ask the Copilot
"what is today's collection?" "show active broadband subscribers"
💵

Billing & Subscriptions

Handles plan activations, invoice generation, overdue list tracking, cancelation, and automated recurring billing status for subscribers.

Ask the Copilot
"who has pending payments?" "which customers paid 210 dollars today?"
👤

Customer Manager

View subscriber records, manage profiles, search by name, phone number, or location, and track status parameters (active, inactive, suspended, archive).

Ask the Copilot
"show inactive customers" "details of Ram Jacob"
🛠

Complaint Desk

Submit and resolve technical complaints, assign service tickets, check ticket status, and summarize pending technical issues.

Ask the Copilot
"how many open complaints do we have?" "give me complaint summary"
